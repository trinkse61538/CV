How I Work

My approach is to diagnose the funnel first, then connect media, tracking, CRM, data, and automation into one measurable growth system.

Diagnose the Funnel

Map the user journey from impression to activation, then identify where spend, traffic, or users are leaking.

Match Intent to Channel

Choose Google, Meta, LinkedIn, email, or CRM workflows based on user intent, product stage, and business KPI.

Track What Matters

Connect GA4, GTM, CRM and campaign data so decisions are based on activation and revenue signals, not only clicks.

Test Creatives & Offers

Build creative hypotheses from user pain points, market context, and funnel stage, then test with clean learning loops.

Automate Repetition

Use automation and AI to reduce manual reporting, speed up insight generation, and keep optimization cycles consistent.
