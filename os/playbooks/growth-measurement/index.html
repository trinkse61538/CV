01 · When to use

Use this system whenever marketing performance must lead to a decision.

The playbook is designed for recurring performance reviews across paid media, lead-generation funnels and growth experiments where teams need to understand whether results are on-track, trustworthy and actionable.

It works for daily operational checks, weekly channel reviews, monthly business reporting and campaign post-mortems. The depth changes, but the logic remains the same: validate the measurement, compare performance to an expectation, diagnose the gap and decide what happens next.

Do not use it as a decorative dashboard framework. The review is incomplete when it presents numbers without an owner, next action or learning loop.

Use this playbook when…
  • Spend and outcomes need a shared source of truth.
  • The team needs plan-versus-actual visibility.
  • Tracking quality may affect interpretation.
  • Campaign changes require evidence and follow-up.

02 · KPI hierarchy

Start with the business outcome, then move downward only to explain it.

A KPI hierarchy prevents channel metrics from being mistaken for business success.

LEVEL 01

Business outcome

Revenue, qualified customers, funded accounts, verified accounts or another result that matters to the business.

Primary truth
LEVEL 02

Funnel outcome

Leads, qualified leads, completed applications, eKYC, activated users or conversion stages between media and business value.

Conversion truth
LEVEL 03

Channel efficiency

Spend, CPA, CPL, conversion rate, ROAS, cost per qualified result and pacing against budget.

Optimization layer
LEVEL 04

Diagnostic metrics

CTR, CPC, CPM, reach, frequency, impression share, landing-page behavior and creative-level signals.

Explanation layer
Review rule: Move downward through the hierarchy to diagnose a problem. Do not optimize a lower-level metric when the higher-level outcome is already healthy.

03 · Required inputs

The review needs context before it needs more metrics.

GOAL

Business objective

The outcome, review window and decision that the campaign is expected to support.

PLAN

Expected performance

Budget, volume, efficiency target, timing and any assumptions behind the plan.

ACTUAL

Observed results

Channel data, lead actuals, qualified outcomes and historical comparison periods.

QUALITY

Measurement confidence

Tracking status, source freshness, attribution limitations and known manual adjustments.

05 · Tracking QA

Do not optimize performance until you know what the measurement can and cannot prove.

EVENT

Conversion event exists

The intended conversion is firing and uses the correct event name, parameters and destination.

COUNT

Volume is plausible

Conversions are neither unexpectedly zero nor implausibly higher than lead or business records.

PATH

Funnel stages connect

Platform conversions, analytics events and actual leads can be reconciled directionally.

FRESHNESS

Latest data is current

The newest row or API response is recent enough for the requested review window.

ATTRIBUTION

Credit rules are understood

The review states whether platform, analytics or CRM attribution is being used.

MANUAL

Overrides are visible

Hardcoded spend, lead adjustments or offline outcomes are clearly marked and documented.

Confidence labels

Classify conclusions explicitly.

ConfirmedDirectly supported by clean source data.
LikelySupported by multiple signals but not fully proven.
PossibleA reasonable hypothesis requiring validation.
UnknownInsufficient data or tracking coverage.

06 · Anomaly rules

A warning should identify the signal, context and first investigation.

PACING

Volume behind elapsed plan

Actual results are materially below the amount expected for the time already passed.

Check delivery, demand and funnel conversion.
EFFICIENCY

Cost rising without outcome growth

Spend or traffic cost increases while leads, quality or revenue remain flat.

Check auction pressure, creative and qualification.
TRACKING

Spend present, conversions missing

The channel is delivering traffic but the expected conversion signal has disappeared.

Check event firing, page changes and data freshness.
QUALITY

Lead volume healthy, downstream weak

Top-of-funnel results look efficient while qualified or completed outcomes deteriorate.

Check targeting, lead intent and follow-up process.
CREATIVE

Response rate declining

CTR or engagement falls while CPM and audience delivery remain broadly stable.

Check fatigue, message fit and creative variation.
MIX

One segment distorts the total

A channel, campaign or audience creates most of the change in aggregate performance.

Break down before changing the whole account.

07 · Optimization decisions

Match the action to the stage where evidence is strongest.

TRACKING ISSUE

Fix measurement first

Do not scale or pause based on a number that cannot be trusted.

DELIVERY ISSUE

Inspect budget, bid and audience availability

Use when spend or reach is materially behind plan before evaluating conversion quality.

RESPONSE ISSUE

Change creative or message

Use when impressions are available but clicks or engagement are weakening.

CONVERSION ISSUE

Inspect landing page and offer

Use when traffic quality appears stable but post-click conversion falls.

QUALITY ISSUE

Refine targeting and qualification

Use when lead volume is healthy but downstream outcomes are weak.

HEALTHY SYSTEM

Scale gradually with guardrails

Increase investment while watching marginal cost, quality and saturation.

08 · Review template

Use one concise brief to close every review.

The template keeps findings, confidence and actions connected.

Reusable review brief
01

Objective

What outcome and time window are being reviewed?

02

Data confidence

Which sources are current, complete and comparable?

03

Headline result

What changed versus plan, prior period or expected pace?

04

Main driver

Which funnel stage, segment or metric explains the result?

05

Decision

What action will be taken, avoided or tested?

06

Follow-up

Who owns the action, when is the next review and what evidence confirms success?

Headline: [Result] is [ahead / on-track / behind] versus [plan / baseline] because [main driver].

Decision: [Action] will be taken by [owner] and reviewed after [time window] using [success signal].

09 · Common failure modes

Most weak reviews fail before the recommendation is written.

01

Optimizing the wrong KPI

A lower-level channel metric improves while the business or funnel outcome does not.

02

Reviewing stale data

The newest source row is old, but the dashboard is treated as current.

03

Confusing platform conversions with actual outcomes

Reported conversions are accepted without reconciling them to lead or business records.

04

Reacting to one-day noise

A normal fluctuation triggers a major account change before the signal is sustained.

05

Listing causes without choosing one

The report produces many possibilities but no prioritized investigation.

06

Ending without a follow-up window

A change is made, but no owner, expected effect or review date is recorded.
